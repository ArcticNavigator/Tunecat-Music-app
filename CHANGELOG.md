# Changelog

All notable, user-visible changes to **Tunecat Music**, newest first.

This file is the single source of truth for release notes: the GitHub release
description is generated from the matching version section here, and the app
shows the same section in its "What's new" screen after an update. When
releasing, add a `## vX.Y.Z — YYYY-MM-DD` section at the top **before** tagging.

## v0.1.2 — 2026-08-15

- **Full playlists — no more 200-song cap.** Playlists, Liked Songs, library
  artists/albums and podcast episode lists now load completely, no matter how
  large they are.
- **Updates now keep your data.** Updating no longer resets the app — your
  sign-in, connected YouTube Music session, settings, downloads and listening
  history all survive updates. (Uninstalling still removes everything cleanly.)
- **You're in control of updates.** When a new version is available, it
  downloads quietly in the background and then asks whether to install now or
  when you close the app — no more surprise restarts.
- **"What's new" screen.** After every update, the app shows you exactly what
  changed (like this list!).

## v0.1.1 — 2026-08-15

- **New name: Tunecat Music.** Same app, fresh identity.
- **Podcasts.** Browse shows by topic and play any episode.
- **Better home screen.** Sections open as full pages (like albums do), with
  your scroll position remembered when you go back. Guest mode now always shows
  playable trending songs.
- **Reliable artwork.** Album and playlist covers load consistently instead of
  showing placeholders.
- **Feedback built in.** Send bug reports and suggestions from Settings —
  guests included.
- **Stay signed in.** Your connected YouTube Music session now refreshes itself
  silently, so your personalized home keeps working day after day.
- Search results gained a back button, and disconnecting YouTube Music now
  correctly returns the home screen to guest mode.

## v0.1.0 — 2026-06-17

- Initial release: search and play anything on YouTube Music, artist and album
  pages, playlists, synced lyrics, downloads, mini-player, Google sign-in with
  your library, and full guest mode.
